RiseKey is operated by the developer identified as the seller on its Apple App Store listing ("RiseKey", "we", or "us"). This policy describes the local-first version of RiseKey for iPhone.

Data collection

RiseKey does not create user accounts and does not collect, transmit, sell, rent, or share personal data. The app does not contain advertising, analytics, tracking, or cloud-database SDKs.

Data stored on your device

RiseKey stores alarm schedules, Wake Key values and labels, mission history, streak and challenge state, onboarding choices, and preferences locally on your iPhone. A limited snapshot containing the next alarm, readiness state, and streak is shared with the RiseKey widget. This information is not transmitted to RiseKey or third parties.

Camera use

RiseKey requests camera access only to recognize QR codes and supported retail barcodes. Recognition happens live on the device. RiseKey does not capture, save, upload, or share photos or videos.

Alarms and notifications

RiseKey uses Apple's AlarmKit to schedule alarms and may use local notifications for Wake Check reminders. These features are managed by iOS and do not send alarm information to RiseKey.

Retention and deletion

Local information remains on the device until you delete it or uninstall RiseKey. Select Settings > Delete all RiseKey data to cancel RiseKey alarms and erase local alarms, Wake Keys, history, challenge, onboarding, and widget state. Camera and alarm permissions can be withdrawn in iPhone Settings.

Children

RiseKey is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If RiseKey's data practices change, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosure will be updated before the changed version is released.

Contact

For privacy or support questions, use the RiseKey support form. Do not include private information or Wake Key values.
