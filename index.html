Troubleshooting

Fast fixes

01

The test does not start

Register a Wake Key first. RiseKey needs a saved code to test the complete alarm-to-scan flow.

02

Alarm access is off

Open iPhone Settings > Apps > RiseKey > Alarms, enable access, and reopen RiseKey.

03

The scanner will not open

Open iPhone Settings > Apps > RiseKey > Camera and enable access.

04

The widget is behind

Open RiseKey after editing an alarm. iOS controls the exact timing of widget refreshes.

Important

Keep a backup while testing.

RiseKey uses Apple's system alarm experience. Apple provides system controls that RiseKey cannot disable. Do not use RiseKey as an emergency or safety-critical alert.

Your data

Local by design.

RiseKey has no account, advertising, analytics, tracking, or cloud upload. Use Settings > Delete all RiseKey data to cancel RiseKey alarms and erase local app data.
